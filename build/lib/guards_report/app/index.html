Every figure is fetched or computed in code. Times are US Eastern.

Generate

Statcast adds the handedness-split zone maps and spray charts; it turns a seconds-long run into a few minutes. Written analysis gives every player a profile, a form read and a matchup, plus the game note — computed from the pitch corpus and the fitted models, not generated. No language model is called, nothing is billed, and the same game yields the same words every time.

MODELS

Checking…
The props and first-five models fit on the season in progress and go stale in days; a refit takes about half a minute. The game-outcome coefficients are fitted on completed seasons and do not move mid-season, so they are left alone unless asked for — that run takes three or four minutes and mostly refreshes the talent estimates. A refit that measures worse than the model it would replace is rejected and the old one kept.
