How this is decided

A price carries two different probabilities and both matter. Break-even is what the price charges — at −110 that is 52.4%, and it is the number a bet has to clear. Book is what the market believes once the margin is removed. The gap between them is the bookmaker's cut, and matching the book's opinion exactly still loses that much on every bet.

Our own number is not used raw. It is pulled toward the market in proportion to how uncertain we are — the same shrinkage this report applies to player talent — so a noisy projection collapses onto the market's and produces nothing. Edge is what survives after both the shrinkage and the margin. z is that edge measured in units of our own standard error, and it is what plays are ranked by. Ranking on the raw gap instead would sort on our own error and surface the games we understand worst.
